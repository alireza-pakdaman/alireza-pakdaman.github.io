At Ontario Tech University I worked at the intersection of technology and the
arts, developing generative AI models for a live opera production.

## What I built

- **AI vocal textures** — generative models producing unique vocal timbres that
  performers and sound designers could weave into the score.
- **Generated stage visuals** — AI-generated visual elements projected during
  the performance, responding to the production's themes.
- Collaborated with a diverse team of artists, musicians, and researchers to
  take experimental models from notebook to stage.

## Why it matters

Most ML coursework ends at a validation metric. This work had a hard, public
deadline — a live audience — which meant the models had to be reliable,
art-directable, and fast enough for production use.
